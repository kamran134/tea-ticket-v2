TICKET REFUND POLICY

Last updated: [TO FILL IN: DATE]

1. GENERAL CONDITIONS

1.1. Refunds for electronic tickets purchased through stolits.art are made in accordance with this Policy.

1.2. A refund may be requested for the entire order or for individual unused tickets included in the order.

1.3. No ticket refund is available after the event has started.

2. REFUND AT THE BUYER'S REQUEST

2.1. If a refund is requested more than 24 hours before the event starts, the price of the returned ticket is refunded in full.

2.2. If a refund is requested 24 hours or less before the event starts, 20% of the price of the returned ticket is withheld. The buyer receives 80% of the ticket price.

2.3. No refund is available after the event has started.

3. PARTIAL REFUNDS

3.1. If several tickets were purchased in one order, the buyer may request a refund for one or more unused tickets.

3.2. The remaining tickets not included in the refund request remain valid subject to the applicable conditions of use.

3.3. After a refund is approved, the relevant ticket becomes invalid and cannot be used for admission.

4. EVENT CANCELLATION

4.1. If Stolitsa Art cancels an event, the price of purchased tickets is refunded in full.

4.2. The buyer does not need to provide a separate reason for the refund.

4.3. The refund is made to the bank card used for payment.

5. EVENT POSTPONEMENT

5.1. If an event is postponed, the ticket automatically remains valid for the new date unless Stolitsa Art states otherwise.

5.2. A buyer who cannot attend on the new date may request a refund.

6. VENUE CHANGE

6.1. If the event venue changes, Stolitsa Art notifies buyers.

6.2. Depending on the circumstances, the buyer may be allowed to use the ticket at the new venue or request a refund.

7. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

7.1. A refund request must be submitted through the contact form on stolits.art.

7.2. The form requires:
name;
email or phone number;
subject;
message.

7.3. Stolitsa Art may request additional information if necessary to process the refund.

7.4. The administrator makes the refund decision after checking the order, ticket status and compliance with this Policy.

8. REFUND METHOD

8.1. Refunds are made to the bank card used to pay for the relevant order.

8.2. The actual time required for the funds to reach the card may depend on the payment service and the card-issuing bank.

9. IMPORTANT PROVISIONS

9.1. An unused ticket cannot be refunded after the event has started.

9.2. A ticket that has been used for admission is not refundable.

9.3. If a visitor is refused admission or removed from an event for violating venue rules or organizer requirements, the ticket price is not refunded.

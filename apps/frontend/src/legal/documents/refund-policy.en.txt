Ticket Refund Policy

StolitsArt Ticket refunds depend on how much time is left before the event and on the payment method.

1. If the event goes ahead as planned

More than 24 hours before the start, the ticket price is refunded in full. 24 hours or less before the start, 20% is withheld: you receive 80%. After the event has started, refunds are not available.

2. If the event is cancelled or postponed

If it is cancelled, you get a full refund. If it is postponed, the ticket remains valid for the new date. If the new date does not work for you, you can request a refund.

3. How to request a refund

Write to support@tea-ticket.com. Money is returned to the card used for payment. The time it takes depends on the bank.

You can refund unused tickets from the same order individually. Once a refund is confirmed, that ticket is no longer valid.

4. When there is no refund

The ticket has already been used for entry. The event has already started. Entry was refused because venue rules were broken.

User Agreement

1. General

StolitsArt Ticket sells electronic tickets for events. By buying a ticket you accept this agreement, the Privacy Policy and the Refund Policy. No registration or account is required.

2. Booking and payment

Selected seats are held for 15 minutes. Payment is made on Kapital Bank's secure page. If you do not pay in time, the hold is released. After a successful payment the ticket is emailed to you and is also available via a link.

To buy a ticket you need a name, phone number and email. Guest names are optional.

3. Ticket and entry

Entry is confirmed with a QR code. You may pass the ticket link to someone else. You are responsible for keeping the link safe.

4. Cancellation and postponement

If an event is cancelled, you get a full refund. If it is postponed, the ticket remains valid for the new date, or you can request a refund under the Refund Policy.

5. Venue rules

Venue rules and the organiser's requirements apply at the event. If you break them, you may be refused entry without a refund.

6. Questions

For orders, payments and tickets, write to support@tea-ticket.com.

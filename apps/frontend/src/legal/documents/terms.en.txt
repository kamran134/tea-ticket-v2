This User Agreement governs the use of stolits.art and the purchase of tickets for events organized by Stolitsa Art.

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1. The website stolits.art is used by Stolitsa Art to provide information about events, make reservations and sell electronic tickets.

1.2. By using the website, making a reservation or purchasing a ticket, the user confirms that they have read the User Agreement, Privacy Policy and Ticket Refund Policy.

1.3. Registration and creation of a personal account are not required to make a purchase.

2. RESERVATIONS AND TICKET PURCHASES

2.1. After selecting tickets, the user may make a reservation.

2.2. Reserved tickets are held for 15 minutes.

2.3. If payment is not completed within the reservation period, the reservation is automatically cancelled and the tickets become available for purchase again.

2.4. If the user starts payment before the reservation period expires, the selected tickets remain held until the payment system provides a final result.

2.5. The reservation link allows the user to return to the order during the reservation period.

2.6. After successful payment, an order and electronic ticket are created.

2.7. To make a purchase, the buyer must provide their first name, last name, phone number and email address. Guest names may be provided at the buyer's discretion.

3. ELECTRONIC TICKET

3.1. The electronic ticket is sent to the email address provided by the buyer.

3.2. The ticket is also available through a link that the user may open on another device or share with another person.

3.3. Admission is confirmed by a valid ticket and QR code.

3.4. The name of the buyer or guest does not have to match the person presenting a valid ticket.

3.5. If a guest name was provided, it may be used when checking the ticket.

3.6. The buyer is responsible for the accuracy of the information provided during the order, including guest information.

4. TICKET CHECKING

4.1. The primary method of checking a ticket is scanning the QR code.

4.2. If necessary, event staff may find the order manually using the buyer's name, phone number or email address, or a provided guest name.

4.3. If a ticket covers several people, each person who actually enters is recorded separately.

4.4. If not all people covered by the order have entered, the remaining unused tickets remain valid until the event starts.

4.5. Once all tickets have been used, further admission using the corresponding ticket/order is not possible.

5. TRANSFER OF TICKETS

5.1. The buyer may transfer a purchased ticket to another person.

5.2. Stolitsa Art does not require the name of the person presenting the ticket to match the buyer's or guest's name if the ticket is valid.

5.3. The buyer is responsible for keeping the ticket link and access information secure.

6. PAYMENT

6.1. Tickets are paid for online by bank card through the connected payment service.

6.2. Card details are entered on the payment service's side. Stolitsa Art does not store full card details unless this is expressly provided for by the payment infrastructure used.

6.3. If a payment requires additional verification, the order may temporarily have a payment verification status.

6.4. In case of a technical payment issue, the user may contact Stolitsa Art through the website contact form.

7. EVENT CANCELLATION AND POSTPONEMENT

7.1. Stolitsa Art may cancel or postpone an event.

7.2. If an event is cancelled, the cost of purchased tickets is refunded in full.

7.3. If an event is postponed, the purchased ticket remains valid for the new date unless Stolitsa Art states otherwise.

7.4. If an event is postponed, the buyer may request a refund in accordance with the Ticket Refund Policy.

7.5. If the venue changes, Stolitsa Art notifies buyers. Depending on the circumstances, the buyer may be allowed to use the ticket at the new venue or request a refund.

8. EVENT RULES

8.1. Visitors must comply with venue rules, organizer requirements and safety rules.

8.2. Stolitsa Art may refuse admission or remove a visitor from an event for violating these rules.

8.3. In such cases, the ticket price is not refunded.

9. TICKET REFUNDS

9.1. Ticket refunds are governed by the separate Ticket Refund Policy published on the website.

9.2. The buyer may request a refund for one or more unused tickets from the same order if permitted by the Ticket Refund Policy.

9.3. No ticket refund is available after the event has started.

10. USER REQUESTS

10.1. For questions concerning orders, payments, tickets, refunds or other matters, users may contact Stolitsa Art through the website contact form.

10.2. The form requires:
name;
email or phone number;
subject;
message.

10.3. Requests are reviewed during business hours.

11. TECHNICAL LIMITATIONS

11.1. Stolitsa Art takes reasonable measures to maintain website availability and service operation.

11.2. Stolitsa Art is not responsible for temporary unavailability of the website, payment service or individual functions due to causes beyond its reasonable control.

12. PERSONAL DATA

12.1. Personal data are processed in accordance with Stolitsa Art's Privacy Policy and the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

12.2. Before placing an order, the user confirms that they have read the User Agreement, Privacy Policy and Ticket Refund Policy.

13.1. This User Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

Privacy Policy

The personal data operator is Stolic Art LLC.

1. What data we collect

When you buy a ticket on StolitsArt Ticket, we receive:
the buyer's name and phone number;
email address;
guest names, if you choose to provide them;
order details: event, seats, price and status;
payment details: amount, status and payment identifier.

No account is required. We do not store bank card details.

2. Why we need it

We use this data to issue the ticket, email it to you, contact you about the order and check you in at the event. We also comply with the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

3. Who we share data with

Payments are processed by Kapital Bank: card details are entered on the bank's side. Ticket emails are sent by Resend.

We do not sell data to advertisers. The administrator can see orders. At the entrance, staff only see what is needed to let you in.

4. Browser

The site remembers your language and theme in the browser. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising pixels.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data. Write to support@tea-ticket.com.
